# AKARA native visual direction

Status: candidate. A concept image is design material, never the running application.

Intent: make a returning Diablo player feel the delight of opening a treasured character editor, with the clarity and precision of a contemporary desktop tool. The equipment itself is the focal point. Editing must feel deliberate and legible.

Signature: an open arch forming an A around one suspended amber seed; quiet, restrained metalwork. Warm ivory editorial typography, matte obsidian surfaces, desaturated antique brass and a small ember accent. Original identity, no borrowed game logos or game art.

Composition: native desktop frame; slim navigation rail; prominent character identity; spacious equipment paperdoll and native-size inventory; a readable right-hand item inspector. Consistent baseline grid, sharp hierarchy, generous breathing room, crisp controls. No wall of equal cards or all-caps microtext.

Protected semantics: exactly two weapon-set choices I/II. Ring slots remain separate; the two-handed mercenary reflection is one item, not a duplicate. All names, values, localization, item state and interaction labels are native runtime text and controls, never baked into production art.

Logo topology: two supporting arch strokes and one central seed. Strong silhouette at32px, no circle of runes, shields, wings, skulls, horns, crown, or ornamental filigree. Wordmark spelled AKARA.

Review crops: logo at32/64px, top-left identity, selected item inspector, inventory cell edges, active navigation, footer actions, small text at100% scale. Inspect structural counts, material continuity, negative space and all authored text.

Language: write primary Spanish directly, with concise commands (Abrir, Revisar cambios, Guardar, Deshacer) and game terms already familiar to the user. Native labels may preserve an item's official name; no pseudo-Latin or decorative invented text.
